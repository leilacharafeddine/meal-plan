# Week 1 of 4 — 19 – 25 October 2026 (Week 43)

_Ovulation tail into early luteal — colourful, balanced meals Mon–Tue, then grounding carbs and magnesium (almonds, oats, beans, dark chocolate). Salmon & alioli on Wednesday._

**Cycle (practice, as if off the pill):** Day 1 = Mon 5 Oct (bleed Mon–Fri), 28-day cycle. Mon–Tue: cycle days 15–16, end of the ovulation window. Wed–Sun: days 17–21, early luteal.

**Rules:** weekday dishes ≤25 min · weekend ≤35 min · soups only in bleed week and only smooth/blended (or noodle broth) · no raw tomato (bar a few cherry), cilantro, chilli, heavy spice blends, canned tuna, lentils · no toaster (toast in the air fryer) · rice in the rice cooker, batch of 2 · Mercadona shopping.

## Monday 19 Oct — GAP (strength) · Cycle day 15: Ovulation (end of window)
Focus: Colourful fruit & veg, antioxidants, balanced plates.

### Breakfast · Greek yoghurt, kiwi, berries, oats & almonds — 5 min
No cooking. Protein after the gym.
Equipment: Bowl
Ingredients: Yogur griego natural 200 g, Kiwi 1, Frutos rojos (frozen, thawed) 60 g, Copos de avena 20 g, Almendras 15 g
- **0–5 min** — Spoon yoghurt into a bowl. Top with sliced kiwi, berries, oats and chopped almonds.

### Lunch · Chicken souvlaki rice, peppers, cucumber & tzatziki — 20 min
Cook 2 portions of chicken (Tuesday dinner wrap) and 2 of rice (Tuesday lunch).
Equipment: Rice cooker, Air fryer
Swaps: No raw tomato — cucumber and pepper instead.
Ingredients: Pechuga de pollo (filetes) 360 g (2 portions), Arroz basmati 150 g (2 portions), Limón ½, Orégano seco 1 tsp, Ajo 1 clove, Aceite de oliva 1 tbsp, Pimiento rojo ½, Pepino ¼, Salsa tzatziki 3 tbsp, Side salad 1 handful
- **0–2 min** — Rinse 150 g rice. Rice cooker, 300 ml water, pinch of salt. Start.
- **2–5 min** — Cube all the chicken. Mix with oil, lemon juice, oregano, grated garlic and salt.
- **5–17 min** — Air fryer, 200°C, 12 min. Shake halfway.
- **5–11 min** — Slice pepper into strips; add to the air fryer for the last 6 min. Dice cucumber.
- **17–20 min** — Plate half the rice, half the chicken, pepper, cucumber, tzatziki, salad. Box the rest of the rice and chicken.

### Dinner · Falafel, hummus & cucumber plate with side salad — 10 min
Air fryer. Quick and light.
Equipment: Air fryer
Ingredients: Falafel (refrigerated) 5, Hummus 3 tbsp, Pepino ¼, Zanahoria 1, Ensalada mezclum 1 handful, Limón squeeze
- **0–8 min** — Air fryer, 190°C, 8 min for the falafel.
- **0–5 min** — Cut cucumber and carrot into sticks. Dress salad with lemon and oil.
- **8–10 min** — Plate falafel, hummus, veg sticks and salad.

## Tuesday 20 Oct — Upper Body (strength) · Cycle day 16: Ovulation (end of window)
Focus: Vitamin C + iron-rich protein; high-energy day.

### Breakfast · Scrambled eggs with spinach on rye toast, kiwi — 8 min
Ten minutes.
Equipment: Air fryer (toast), Frying pan
Ingredients: Huevos 2, Espinacas baby 1 handful, Pan de centeno 1 slice, Mantequilla 5 g, Kiwi 1
- **0–2 min** — Air fryer, 200°C, 3 min for the bread. Melt butter in a pan on medium-low.
- **2–4 min** — Wilt the spinach in the pan, 1 min.
- **4–8 min** — Add beaten eggs. Stir slowly until just set. Season. Serve on toast with kiwi.

### Lunch · Steak fajita rice with peppers & onion — 20 min
Steak + peppers = iron with vitamin C. Uses Monday's rice.
Equipment: Frying pan, Microwave
Swaps: Mild seasoning: sweet paprika, garlic, oregano. No chilli, no cumin. Parsley instead of coriander.
Ingredients: Filete de ternera (añojo) 150 g, Pimentón dulce 1 tsp, Ajo en polvo ½ tsp, Orégano ½ tsp, Lima 1, Aceite de oliva 1 tbsp, Pimientos (tricolor) 1, Cebolla roja ½, Leftover rice (Monday) 1 portion, Perejil small handful
- **0–3 min** — Mix paprika, garlic powder, oregano, salt, ½ lime juice and oil. Coat the steak.
- **3–10 min** — Slice pepper and onion. Fry in a hot pan 6 min until charred. Set aside.
- **10–16 min** — High heat. Sear steak 2–3 min per side. Rest 3 min.
- **16–18 min** — Microwave leftover rice 1½ min, covered, splash of water.
- **18–20 min** — Slice steak thin. Plate rice, veg, steak. Squeeze lime, add parsley.

### Dinner · Leftover chicken wrap with tzatziki & cucumber — 5 min
Uses Monday's chicken. No cooking.
Equipment: Frying pan, Microwave
Ingredients: Leftover souvlaki chicken 1 portion, Tortilla de trigo / pan de pita 1, Salsa tzatziki 2 tbsp, Pepino ¼, Ensalada mezclum 1 handful
- **0–2 min** — Microwave chicken 1 min. Warm the wrap in a dry pan 30 s per side.
- **2–5 min** — Spread tzatziki, add leaves, cucumber, chicken. Roll.

## Wednesday 21 Oct — V-Burn (strength) · Cycle day 17: Luteal (early)
Focus: Complex carbs, magnesium, grounding meals.

### Breakfast · Cocoa overnight oats with kefir, banana & almonds — 5 min
Make Tuesday night. Oats, cocoa and almonds = magnesium.
Equipment: Jar (fridge overnight)
Ingredients: Copos de avena 45 g, Kéfir 150 ml, Yogur griego 2 tbsp, Cacao puro en polvo 1 tsp, Plátano ½, Almendras 15 g

- **0–3 min** — Top with sliced banana and chopped almonds.

### Lunch · Salmon with alioli, baby potatoes & green beans — 22 min
Your favourite. Cook 2 salmon fillets — the second is Thursday's dinner.
Equipment: Air fryer
Ingredients: Lomos de salmón 2 (≈250 g), Patata baby / patata pequeña 250 g, Judías verdes 100 g, Alioli 1 tbsp, Limón ½, Perejil pinch, Aceite de oliva 1 tbsp
- **0–3 min** — Halve the potatoes. Toss with oil and salt.
- **3–21 min** — Air fryer, 200°C, 18 min. Shake at 9 min.
- **11–21 min** — At 11 min: add both salmon fillets (skin down, salted) and the green beans. Cook the last 10 min together.
- **21–22 min** — Plate 1 salmon, potatoes, beans. Big spoon of alioli, lemon, parsley. Fridge the 2nd fillet.

### Dinner · Kale green sauce rigatoni with white beans — 18 min
Light. Kale + beans = magnesium, folate, iron.
Equipment: Large pot, Small pan, Hand blender + tall jug
Swaps: No chilli. White beans added for protein.
Ingredients: Rigatoni / macarrones 80 g, Kale 100 g, Ajo 1 clove, Aceite de oliva 1½ tbsp, Limón ½, Parmesano rallado 15 g, Alubias blancas cocidas (bote) 100 g
- **0–2 min** — Boil salted water. Gently fry the garlic in the oil until golden. Tip into a tall jug.
- **2–6 min** — Blanch kale 3 min. Lift into the jug with a splash of water. Add lemon and salt. Blitz with the hand blender until smooth.
- **6–16 min** — Cook pasta in the same water. Add rinsed beans for the last 2 min.
- **16–18 min** — Drain. Stir in kale sauce and parmesan.

Tofu = magnesium + iron. Uses Thursday's rice.

Swaps: No chilli — honey-soy glaze.
Ingredients: Tofu firme 200 g, Maicena 1 tbsp, Salsa de soja 1½ tbsp, Miel 1 tbsp, Vinagre de arroz o de manzana 1 tsp, Leftover rice (Thursday) 1 portion, Pepino ¼, Cebolleta 1, Sésamo 1 tsp

- **18–20 min** — Toss hot tofu in the sauce. Microwave leftover rice 1½ min, covered.

Ten minutes.

- **7–9 min** — Serve with dressed salad.
